# HoneyBoo Sample Videos Directory

This directory stores test and sample video recordings for testing the optical computer vision and tracking pipeline.

## Pre-Packaged Synthetic Sample Video
- **`entrance_sample.mp4`**: A synthetic 8-second 25 FPS hive entrance video generated with `backend/app/utils/synthetic_generator.py`. Contains a hive landing board and realistic simulated bee flight trajectories entering and leaving the hive.

You can also place recorded hive entrance MP4 or AVI footage here for quick testing and demonstrations.
